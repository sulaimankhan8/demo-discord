   * P95 and P99 latencies stabilized significantly.

3. **Faster Failure Detection**

   * Sessions ended quickly instead of degrading slowly.

4. **Clearer Capacity Envelope**

Attempt 34 established the **baseline for stable WebSocket-only testing.**
